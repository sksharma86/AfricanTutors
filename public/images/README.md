# Image Sources & Licensing Status

## `student-tutoring-session.jpg`, `tutor-portrait.jpg`

- **Source:** AI-generated (via the development environment's image
  generation tool), created specifically for this project in August 2026.
  They do not depict real people, are not sourced from any stock photo
  library, and were not scraped from search results or any third-party
  website.
- **Licensing status:** Because they were generated fresh for this
  project (not copied from an existing copyrighted work), there is no
  known third-party copyright claim on them. They are safe to use as
  **development placeholders**.
- **Action required before public launch:** These are placeholders, not
  final brand photography. Before African Tutors launches publicly, the
  owner should replace these with either (a) professionally commissioned
  photography of real students/families and real tutors who have signed
  model releases, or (b) photography properly licensed for commercial web
  use from a reputable stock provider (e.g. Getty, Adobe Stock, iStock)
  with a license that covers the intended use. See `DECISIONS.md` and
  `TODO.md` for the tracked follow-up item.
- **Brief used to generate them:** warm, natural, editorial-style
  photography of (1) an African American student engaged in an online
  tutoring session at home, and (2) a professional African tutor on a
  video call in a modern home office — both avoiding stereotypical,
  poverty-related, or overly staged corporate-stock imagery, per the
  visual direction in `PROJECT_SPEC.md`.
