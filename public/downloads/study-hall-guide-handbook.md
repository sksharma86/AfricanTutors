# Study Hall Guide handbook

Study Hall (at home)

This handbook is for people who have applied to be a Study Hall Guide. Reading it does not approve your application. You cannot set hours until a manager approves you.

## The role

A Study Hall is a private, live hour. The child does their own schoolwork. You are the calm structure in the room: a start, check-ins, and a finish.

You are not tutoring. You do not teach a lesson, give homework answers, or take over the work.

Between real check-ins there is room for your own quiet work. Stay present for the booked hour. No homework is not a reason to end early.

## How an hour runs

1. Plan, at the start. Ask what they are working on, what to start with, and what they would like to finish. Help them set a simple plan. Do not lecture.
2. Focus, during the hour. Stay on task, redirect distractions gently, encourage effort, and move to the next task when it fits. Supervise. Do not tutor.
3. Finish, near the end. Ask what was completed, what remains, and what should happen next. Then complete the required Guide report.

## If nothing is due

1. Check first: school portal, homework planner, backpack or folders, unfinished classwork, upcoming assignments.
2. If nothing is due right now: review notes, organize materials, prepare for a quiz or test, read assigned material, or work on a long-term assignment.
3. If there is genuinely nothing academic: use a quiet, productive study activity that fits Study Hall, and stay for the booked time.

## After you are approved

A manager reviews applications outside this form, including a conversation on WhatsApp. Approval is a separate step. Only then does this account become your Guide workspace, where you set the hours families can book.

The door opens five minutes before a Study Hall starts. If a parent is needed during the hour, use Call Parent in the session. Their phone number stays private.

Questions about your application wait until the team writes to you. Do not set a schedule before you are approved.
